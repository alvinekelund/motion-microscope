Two different signals. The motion you see magnified — breathing, a faint pulse — is amplified movement. The BPM is not from that movement. It's read separately from ~1% changes in your skin's colour as blood flows through it: invisible to your eye, but measurable across thousands of pixels averaged over time. The number and the pulsing are independent measurements that agree — the colour method is rPPG (MIT, 2010).
